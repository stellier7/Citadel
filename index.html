About this development

A modular, two-unit duplex on Roatán — Citadel's first pilot property. Built for short-term rental through Citadel.rent, with a design intended to be replicated across future sites once the model proves out.

Why this property

Small enough to fund quickly, standardized enough to template for the next ten. Prospera's ZEDE framework gives Citadel a clear legal path to issue the raise without waiting on slower jurisdictions.

Total development cost

$200,000 — $100,000 raised via tokens, $100,000 via a BTC-collateralized loan from Tower Bank.

Projected rental yield

18–22% annualized, based on comparable short-term rental performance on Roatán, distributed monthly in USDC.

Exit options

Sell your token position on the secondary market at any time, or hold for ongoing rental income.

Legal structure

Owned by a single-asset LLC formed under Citadel LLC (Prospera ZEDE). Token holders hold a membership interest in this LLC, proportional to tokens held.

Custody

Funds remain in the asset LLC's on-chain wallet through the raise. Once escrow conditions are met, funds are released to the LLC's bank account for construction draws.
